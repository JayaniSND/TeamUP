# BASELINE — Sponsorship Agent

**Agent name:** `sponsorship`
**Agent address:** `agent1q...` ← paste the address printed on boot

Finds a realistic brand fit and drafts cold outreach — **approval-gated**.

## What it does
Over the **Agent Chat Protocol**, reads the athlete's profile, recent results,
metrics, and media notes, and uses Claude to suggest one sponsor fit plus a
short draft outreach email. It records the opportunity with status `drafted`.
It **never sends** — the athlete reviews, edits, and approves first.

## How to use it
Send a note like:

> "Just won the regional open, want to find a local recovery brand to partner with."

It replies with a brand suggestion, a fit score and reason, and a draft email
for your review.
